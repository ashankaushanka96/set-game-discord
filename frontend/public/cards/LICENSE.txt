Card face images in this folder (suit-rank.png) are PNG renderings of
"Vectorized Playing Cards 1.3" by Chris Aguilar
(http://code.google.com/p/vectorized-playing-cards/).

They are free software under the GNU Lesser General Public License, version 3
or (at your option) any later version: see COPYING.LESSER.txt and COPYING.txt.
README-cards.txt and AUTHORS.txt are the library's own notices.

The only change made is converting each card's SVG to a 360px-wide PNG.
The original SVG source files are published in the cardsJS package
(https://www.npmjs.com/package/cardsJS, folder "cards", version 1.1.1).
